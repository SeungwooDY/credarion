## The workflow

On the mismatch page, every unresolved mismatch row gets a flag button (⚑)
next to Resolve. Clicking it opens a popup that requires a **reason** —
Enter submits, Shift+Enter adds a line. The row then shows an amber
"Marked" badge (hover shows the reason), and the reason flows into the
spreadsheet view's CSV export, which is what gets opened in Google Sheets.

Marking is orthogonal to resolving: a marked row stays open until resolved.
Clicking the (now amber) flag again unmarks it.
